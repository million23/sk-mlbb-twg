# Informed Consent Form

## MLBB Tournament Participation

I hereby voluntarily agree to participate in the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) Tournament organized by the Sangguniang Kabataan and PINTIG.

I fully understand and acknowledge the following terms and conditions:

## 1. Voluntary Participation

I confirm that my participation in this tournament is voluntary, and I am fully responsible for my actions during the event.

## 2. Player Eligibility

To be eligible to participate, every player must satisfy all of the following requirements:

1. Must be a **resident of Phase 4, Phase 9, or Phase 10** of Barangay 176.
2. Must be **fifteen (15) years old or above** on **August 29, 2026**, the official opening day of the tournament.
3. Professional Mobile Legends players are **not eligible** to participate.
4. The tournament is intended exclusively for **amateur and community-based players**.
5. Players must register using their **own Mobile Legends account**. Account sharing or piloting is strictly prohibited.
6. Tournament Officials reserve the right to require proof of age and residency at any time before or during the tournament.
7. Players are required to provide their own device and internet connection.

## 3. Team Eligibility

Each participating team shall comply with the following requirements:

1. A team shall consist of **five (5) main players** and may register up to **one (1) substitute player**.
2. All registered players must be residents of **Phase 4, Phase 9, or Phase 10**.
3. Every team must have **at least one (1) player who is a resident of Phase 9 (Barangay 176-E).**
4. Each team must designate a Team Captain who is a resident of Phase 9.
5. All players must be **15 years old or older** on the first day of the tournament.
6. Once registration has officially closed, no additional players may be added or replaced except with approval of Tournament Officials.
7. Players are required to provide their own device and internet connection.

## 4. Tournament Structure

### Elimination Stage

**Date:** August 29–30, 2026

- Maximum of **64 Teams**
- Teams shall be divided into **four (4) brackets**.
- All matches shall be played in a **Best-of-Three (BO3)** format.
- Winners advance to the Playoffs.

### Playoffs

**Date:** August 31, 2026

- The **eight (8) qualified teams** from the Elimination Stage shall advance to the Playoffs.
- All Playoff matches shall be played in a **Best-of-Three (BO3)** format.

### Semi-Finals

**Date:** August 31, 2026

- Semi-Final matches shall also follow a **Best-of-Three (BO3)** format.

### Grand Finals

**Date:** August 31, 2026

- The Championship Match shall be played in a **Best-of-Five (BO5)** series.

## 5. Tournament Administration

1. Tournament Officials shall determine:
   - Match schedules
   - Bracket assignments
   - Lobby creation
   - Side selection
   - Draft procedures
   - Technical rulings
   - Replay requests
   - Penalties and sanctions
2. Decisions made by Tournament Officials shall be **final, binding, and non-appealable**.
3. Tournament Officials reserve the right to amend these Rules when necessary to preserve fairness and competitive integrity.

## 6. Rules and Regulations

I agree to abide by all the rules, guidelines, and decisions set by the tournament organizers. Any violation may result in penalties or disqualification.

### Match Readiness

- Players must be inside the tournament lobby **at least five (5) minutes** before the scheduled match.
- Teams failing to appear within **ten (10) minutes** after the official match time shall automatically forfeit the match.

### Lobby Creation

- Tournament Officials shall create the lobby.
- Only the registered Team Captain shall receive the lobby invitation.

### Draft Pick

- The team positioned on the **upper side of the tournament bracket** shall receive the **first pick** during the first game.
- Side selection for succeeding games shall follow the Tournament Officials' instructions.

### Drafting Rules

- Official MLBB Tournament Draft Mode shall be used.
- Standard **3-ban drafting system** shall apply.
- If a player disconnects during drafting, drafting shall continue.
- The disconnected player may reconnect before the match officially begins.

## 7. In-Game Rules

1. Use of cheats, scripts, map hacks, modified game files, custom skins, third-party software, or unauthorized applications is strictly prohibited.
2. Intentional feeding, griefing, AFK, trolling, or disruptive gameplay is prohibited.
3. Chat shall only be used for necessary team communication.
4. Excessive trash talking, hate speech, discriminatory language, or abusive communication is prohibited.
5. If a player disconnects during the match, gameplay shall continue unless Tournament Officials approve a Technical Pause.
6. Each team is allowed **two (2) Technical Pauses** per match with a maximum duration of **five (5) minutes**.
   1. The Tournament Committee may pause the game for unforeseen circumstances beyond the team's technical pauses.
7. Failure to reconnect within the allotted pause period shall require the affected team to continue the match.

## 8. Code of Conduct

I commit to demonstrate sportsmanship, respect, and fair play towards fellow participants, organizers, and officials throughout the tournament.

### Sportsmanship

Participants shall treat fellow competitors, teammates, Tournament Officials, organizers, and spectators with courtesy and respect.

The following conduct is prohibited:

- Personal insults
- Harassment
- Unsportsmanlike behavior
- Provocation
- Disrespectful gestures

### In-Game Communication

Normal gameplay, hero emotes, celebrations, and standard game mechanics are permitted.

### Recall Spamming

Intentional and excessive Recall spamming intended to provoke or disrespect opponents may result in:

- Official Warning
- Game Penalty
- Match Forfeiture
- Disqualification for repeated violations

## 9. Fair Play Policy

The following are considered Major Offenses.

### Match Fixing

Participants shall not intentionally manipulate or influence the outcome of any match. Any agreement involving match-fixing, intentional losing, or bribery is strictly prohibited.

### Duty to Report

Any participant aware of match-fixing shall immediately report the matter to Tournament Officials. Failure to report may constitute participation or complicity.

### Spectator Monitoring

Players shall not watch livestreams, observer feeds, or receive outside information during an ongoing match.

### Unauthorized Software

Use of any unauthorized software, hacks, scripts, overlays, exploits, macros, or modified game clients is prohibited.

### Bug Exploitation

Knowingly abusing game bugs or glitches to gain competitive advantage is prohibited. Players discovering significant bugs shall immediately report them to Tournament Officials.

### Account Sharing (Piloting)

Only officially registered players may participate. Piloting, account sharing, or substitute players using another player's account shall result in immediate disqualification.

### Account Integrity

All accounts must comply with the Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Terms of Service. Accounts under suspension, investigation, or involved in fraudulent activity are ineligible.

### 9.1 Mid-Match Disqualification

If a Participant is disqualified during an ongoing match due to a major violation:

- The current game shall be forfeited.
- Remaining games in the series shall also be forfeited.
- No substitute player shall be permitted.

## 10. Prohibited Behavior

The following conduct shall not be tolerated:

### Harassment and Discrimination

Zero tolerance shall apply to harassment, bullying, discrimination, hate speech, racism, sexism, or any offensive conduct.

### Gambling

Betting or wagering on any tournament match is strictly prohibited.

### Bribery

Offering, requesting, or accepting money, gifts, favors, or incentives intended to influence match results is prohibited.

### Threats and Violence

Threatening or committing physical violence toward Players, Officials, Organizers, or Spectators shall result in immediate removal from the tournament.

### Harassment of Tournament Officials

Participants shall not intimidate, verbally abuse, or harass Tournament Officials. Failure to comply with official instructions may result in disciplinary action.

3. Any matter not specifically addressed shall be resolved by the Tournament Committee in the interest of fairness, integrity, and sportsmanship.
4. The Tournament Committee reserves the right to amend these Rules prior to or during the event when necessary to ensure the orderly conduct of the competition.
